Effective Setpoint Formula — Cool Mode
Eff Cool Spt = Base Temp Spt + User Adjust
When room temperature climbs above the comfort deadband, the box enters Cool Mode. The effective setpoint shifts upward by 2°F from the base, signaling the system to maximize cold airflow into the space. The reheat coil stays off.
  • 🔵
    Damper opens to deliver maximum cold supply air from RTU
  • 🔵
    Reheat coil remains OFF — no heating energy used
  • 🔵
    Airflow (cfm) rises as damper opens further
  • 🔵
    DAT will be close to RTU supply temp — no reheat boost

Effective Setpoint Formula — Reheat Mode
Eff Heat Spt = Base Temp Spt - User Adjust
When room temperature drops below the comfort deadband, the box enters Reheat Mode. The effective setpoint shifts downward by 2°F from the base. The reheat coil fires to warm the cold supply air before delivering it to the space.
  • 🟠
    Damper may reduce to minimum position to limit cold air volume
  • 🟠
    Reheat coil turns ON — Heat Stg 1 = Enabled
  • 🟠
    DAT will rise above RTU supply temp as coil fires
  • 🟠
    If DAT is NOT rising, suspect a reheat coil or valve issue
