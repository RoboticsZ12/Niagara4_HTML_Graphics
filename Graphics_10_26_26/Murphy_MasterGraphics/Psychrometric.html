Murphy Control Psychrometric Chart

Chart Settings

Live ORD data is not available on this page (Baja not loaded). Manual entry still works.
Dry bulb plus one other value defines a point. The selected dot marks that second input. Type into any other row, or click its dot, to make it the input instead; the rest are calculated. Every input row has an ORD box: leave it blank to type a value, or paste an ORD to subscribe to the live value. ORDs for humidity ratio, enthalpy, specific volume and vapor pressure are read in the chart units. Defaults are 72 °F and 50% RH at sea level (the middle of the ASHRAE 55 comfort zone). Set a Time on a point and its ORDs are read from history for that moment instead of live. Use a history: ORD (history:/Station/Point), or a point that has a history extension. Use the copy button on a point and change its time to build a trend. A point only gets a label on the chart if you give it a title.
